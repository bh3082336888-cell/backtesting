## Global Constraints

**Constants**
- u: `lce_pct` u = 0.01169 × price (the signal bar's close for cloud slopes, the last close
  before D for levels). `lce_atr` u = Wilder ATR(14) of completed session days.
- Distances in u: step 0.303, zone 0.103, big_bar 0.303. Grid search window [0.8, 1.2] × step.
- Clouds: EMA 20/50 on 5m; multipliers M = {5m: 1, 15m: 3, 30m: 6, 1h: 12, 2h: 24}. Slope in u
  per hour = (mid[t] − mid[t − M])/u × 12/M.
- Profiles: VRVP has 200 rows over the last `vp_days` session days before D; SVP has 100 rows per
  session day. HVN = max within ±2 rows and ≥ the 70th percentile. POC tolerance ± zone·u/2.

**Clock**
- Session: entries only when the signal bar's close is 09:40–15:45 New York time, Mon–Fri. EXIT
  when a bar closes at or after 15:55, or outside [09:40, 15:55) on a weekday, or on a weekend.
- Session day runs 18:00 → 18:00 New York time.
- US daylight saving: second Sunday of March 07:00 UTC to first Sunday of November 06:00 UTC is
  UTC−4; otherwise UTC−5.

**Plugin values**
- `params = {"ema_fast": 20, "ema_slow": 50, "vp_days": 5, "step": 0.303, "zone": 0.103,
  "big_bar": 0.303, "theta": 0.01, "wait_bars": 6, "be_frac": 0.5, "max_trades": 2, "atr_n": 14,
  "unit": "pct"}` (`"atr"` in `lce_atr`).
- `grid = {"vp_days": [5, 10], "theta": [0.01, 0.02]}`. `timeframes = ["5m"]`.
- `session = {"tz": "America/New_York", "days": [0, 1, 2, 3, 4], "start": "09:40", "end": "15:45"}`.

**Limits**
- `prepare` ≤ 0.30 s median in a sandbox worker, on BTCUSDT's full research 5m bars, at
  `vp_days = 10`. If it misses, **stop and report**; don't change the budget or the spec.
- Before the trials, no look at real data may compute returns, P&L, hit rate or R.
- Exactly two trials: `lab judge lce_pct --tf 5m`, then `lab judge lce_atr --tf 5m`. Never
  `lab backtest`.
- Nothing in this plan reads the holdout.

**Git**
- One concern per commit. Tests ship in the same commit as the behaviour.
- Commit trailers: `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 (1M context) <noreply@anthropic.com>` and
  `Claude-Session: https://claude.ai/code/session_01WZgKtXA9TNg2AeUCiUM4ii`.

## Review Focus

1. **Daylight-saving switch days:** the entry window and the 15:55 exit must follow New York time
   on the Monday after each switch, and on a Sunday-evening session start. Tested in Task 2 and
   Task 8.
2. **A missing 15:55 bar (a gap):** the position must exit at the first bar after it and never be
   held overnight or over a weekend. Tested in Task 8.
3. **Warm-up and late listings:** with fewer than `vp_days` complete days, or no ATR yet, there
   are no levels, every bracket is NaN, and nothing enters. Tested in Task 6 and Task 7.
4. **Price outside the day's grid** (a gap beyond the outermost level): bracket NaN, no entry, no
   exception. Tested in Task 7.
5. **Flat or zero-volume days** (high == low, volume 0, a dead tail): no division by zero, no
   inf/NaN rows, no levels that day. Tested in Task 6.

### Task 6: Levels

**Files:**
- Create: `strategies/lce_pct.py`. The docstring's first line is the spec's `lce_pct` hypothesis.
  A class `LcePct(Strategy)` carries the global params, grid, timeframes and session; it gets its
  methods in Task 8, and until then `prepare` returns the level features only.
- Test: `tests/test_lce.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `profile(lo, hi, vol, lo_edge, hi_edge, rows) -> np.ndarray`. Each bar's volume is split into
    4 sub-points at (i + 0.5)/4 of [lo, hi], each carrying vol/4, and binned with `np.bincount`.
    This is the vectorisation the spec leaves to the builder; a test bounds it against the exact
    even spread. Returns zeros when hi_edge ≤ lo_edge.
  - `day_levels(lo, hi, vol, day_start: np.ndarray, d: int, vp_days: int, step_u: float, zone_u: float) -> tuple[np.ndarray, bool]`:
    the sorted level centres for session day index d, plus whether the anchor fell back. Returns
    an empty array if fewer than `vp_days` complete days precede d, or if the window has no
    volume or no range.
  - `level_features(bars: pd.DataFrame, u_d: np.ndarray, p: dict) -> pd.DataFrame` (`u_d` from
    `u_day`). Columns:
    - `z_dn_c`: centre of the highest zone with centre ≤ close
    - `z_dn2_c`: the zone below it
    - `z_up_lo`: lower edge of the lowest zone whose lower edge > close
    - `z_up_c`: centre of the lowest zone with centre ≥ close
    - `z_up2_c`: the zone above it
    - `z_dn_hi`: upper edge of the highest zone whose upper edge < close
    - `at_zone`, `touched` (`wait_bars` window), `anchor_fb`, `spacing_u`: the median gap between
      the day's levels inside the VRVP range, in u
  - `u_day(bars, unit, atr_n) -> np.ndarray`: per bar, the u of its session day, from bars
    before that day only. For "pct": 0.01169 × the last close before the day starts. For "atr":
    Wilder ATR(atr_n) of the session-day OHLC of completed days.

- **Grid algorithm.** This is the step the timing spike shows can bust the budget. Precompute for
  each VRVP row j:
  - `nxt_up[j]` = argmax of the profile over the rows whose centres lie in
    [c_j + 0.8·step_u, c_j + 1.2·step_u]
  - `nxt_dn[j]` mirrored

  Use `numpy.lib.stride_tricks.sliding_window_view`; ties go to the lowest row; −1 when no row
  centre falls in the window. Walk from the anchor row through these pointers (Python lists).
  Beyond the profile, continue with `np.arange` multiples of step_u until 10 levels lie past each
  edge.
